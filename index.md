# TIH Sourcing

TIH Sourcing is a private, internal tool operated by Tadas Čekavičius for the recruitment agency Team In-House. It helps recruiters build candidate longlists for open positions.

## What it does

- Reads the position brief that a recruiter enters into a TIH Sourcing Google Sheet.
- Searches public web search results for publicly listed professional profiles.
- Writes a ranked list of candidates back into the same Google Sheet for the recruiter to review. A person decides who is relevant and who is contacted; the tool contacts no one.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only invited users can use it.
