# Privacy policy - TIH Sourcing

Last updated: 2 October 2026

TIH Sourcing is a private, internal tool operated by Tadas Čekavičius (contact: tadascekavicius@gmail.com) for the recruitment agency Team In-House.

## Google account data

The tool signs in with the operator's own Google account and uses two permissions:

- **Google Sheets (read and edit):** to read the position details a recruiter enters in a TIH Sourcing spreadsheet, to write the candidate list and status back into the same spreadsheet, and to read the recruiters' existing longlist spreadsheet so that people already on it are not suggested again.
- **Google Drive (read only):** to find TIH Sourcing spreadsheets shared with the operator and to read a position brief document when a recruiter links one.

The tool does not change or delete any other files. Google user data is used only to provide the functions described above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. It is not shared with third parties except the processors listed below, and only as needed to provide these functions.

## Other data the tool processes

Publicly available search-engine previews of professional profiles (name, headline, employer, location, link). The tool does not log in to LinkedIn or collect data from it directly, and it contacts no one.

## Processors

- **Serper.dev** receives the search queries.
- **Anthropic (Claude API)** receives the position brief, the recruiter's feedback rules and the search previews, in order to extract and score them.

## Storage and retention

Working files are stored on the operator's computer and in the Google Sheets belonging to the recruiters. Local working files older than 90 days are deleted. Cached search results are kept for at most 30 days.

## Your rights

To ask what data is held about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, write to tadascekavicius@gmail.com. Requests are answered within 30 days.
